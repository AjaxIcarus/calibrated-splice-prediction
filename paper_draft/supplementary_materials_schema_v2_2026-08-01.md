# Supplementary methods

## Target population and cache reconstruction

The schema-v2 evaluation included every test sequence and excluded all-zero padding before non-splice sampling. Every valid acceptor and donor position was retained. Valid non-splice positions were represented by a fixed reservoir sample and assigned a reconstruction weight equal to the number of valid non-splice positions divided by the number sampled. This reconstructs the population of valid positions in the GRCh38/MANE evaluation records; it does not represent every intergenic base in complete chromosomes.

## Paired bootstrap design

Within each trained model, 200 bootstrap replicates were generated by stratified position-level resampling within the three true classes. The same resampled indices were applied to all calibration methods, so method contrasts were paired. Percentile intervals quantify uncertainty over the evaluated positions conditional on the fitted checkpoint and calibration parameters. They do not estimate variation across training seeds, genes, transcripts, or chromosomes.

## Reliability-bin support

Reliability curves use 15 equal-width bins. Curves connect only nonempty bins, so visual line length and jaggedness must not be interpreted as equal evidential support. The binary acceptor and donor panels contain bins with very small reconstructed target-population mass, particularly at higher probabilities. Their raw gap can be visually large while their contribution to target-weighted ECE remains small.

For each seed, the machine-readable reliability data retain the method, reliability type, class, bin limits, raw count, reconstructed target weight, target-weight fraction, mean predicted probability, observed frequency or accuracy, absolute gap, and ECE contribution. These records belong in Supplementary Data rather than a printed table because five methods, three reliability views, and 15 bins produce hundreds of rows per model.

## Developmental flank-80 workflow

The seed-11/epoch-2 flank-80 configuration was used during development to establish memory-safe model training and true-logit extraction. It was later re-extracted with schema-v2 rules. The audit confirmed complete coverage of 18,486 validation sequences and 84,258 test sequences, exclusion of 3,413,758 validation and 14,011,378 test padding positions, and the same valid test label census used for flank-400.

The earlier flank-80 calibration results were generated before schema v2, and the full five-method calibration plus paired-bootstrap analysis was not repeated on the corrected caches. Those point estimates and figures are therefore not included as final quantitative evidence. The study does not estimate the causal effect of context length.
